## 📌 1 — Welcome to the Chaos

Standard Sleeper scoring runs the matchup. On top of that, the **Chaos layer**
rewards the absurd and penalizes dead roster spots.

report**. Read the pinned rules so you know which is which.

until every game is fully charted (drops, snaps, stats), which takes ~48 hours.

## 📌 2 — Automated Bonuses (no action needed)

Calculated from official NFL data every Wednesday:

- **+15 Offensive Tackle** — a started offensive player (QB/RB/FB/WR/TE) makes a
  tackle (solo or assist). Defensive tackles never count.
- **+5 Dropped Pass** — a started player is charted with a drop.
- **+5 Red Zone Turnover** — every started player involved in a turnover inside
  the opponent's 20: the one who committed it (QB's INT or the fumbler) **and**
  any started defender who caused it (interception, forced fumble, recovery).
- **−15 Invalid Roster Spot** — a started offensive skill player with **< 15% of
  snaps AND zero touches** (a wasted slot).

You don't report these — the script handles them.

## 📌 3 — Invalid Roster Spot: exemptions & fair play

The −15 targets *unavailable* players, not unlucky ones.

- **Injured players are auto-exempt** — anyone ruled **Out or Doubtful** (or hurt
  in-game) is excluded automatically.
- **Benched players are exempt** (see the Benching rule) — a benching is a +20
  reward, never also a −15. Report benchings so I can exempt them.
- Exempted players are listed each week with the reason.

⚠️ **No Intentional Invalid Lineups.** Leaving an invalid spot on purpose to game
the system = **you forfeit that week's matchup.**

## 📌 7 — 🪑 The Benching Rule (+20)

**+20 if a starter loses their starting role due to poor performance.** Report it
— this is also what exempts the player from the −15 invalid penalty.

**Qualifies if ANY one of these happens:**

1. Coach/coordinator/team officially states the benching was performance-based.
2. Replaced during a competitive game and doesn't return despite being healthy and available.
3. Loses the starting job before the next game, with the team publicly citing performance.
4. Designated inactive for the next game due to performance.

**Does NOT qualify:** injury · illness · concussion protocol · load management ·
resting starters · Week 18 shenanigans · blowout pulls · suspension · planned QB
packages · rotational substitutions.
